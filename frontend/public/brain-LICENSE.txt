Pantip Sentiment procedural brain model

File: brain.glb
Source: original procedural artwork created for the Pantip Sentiment project.
The geometry comes from the same mathematical surfaces used to render the
project's brain_scan_poster.png and brain_scan_loop.mp4. This GLB stores
the two cerebral hemispheres, two cerebellar lobes, and brainstem as
indexed triangle meshes with surface normals. The vertical axis is Y.
