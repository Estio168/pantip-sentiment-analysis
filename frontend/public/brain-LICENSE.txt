Pantip Analytics procedural brain model

Files: brain.glb and brain-poster.png
Source: original procedural artwork generated by assets/generate_brain.mjs.
The GLB contains two cerebral hemispheres, two cerebellar lobes, and a brainstem
as indexed triangle meshes. The vertical axis is Y. The poster is a browser
render of this model using the holographic shader in BrainScene.jsx.
